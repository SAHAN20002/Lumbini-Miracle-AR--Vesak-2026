The Birth of Prince Siddhartha

In the sacred garden of Lumbini, Queen Mahamaya gave birth to Prince Siddhartha. Buddhist tradition describes this moment as miraculous and divine. The newborn child took seven steps, and with each step a lotus flower bloomed beneath his feet, symbolizing the path to enlightenment.
